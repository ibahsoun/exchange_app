# Exchange Center — User Manual

A quick guide to running daily operations on the Exchange Center platform.

---

## Getting Around

The **sidebar** on the left is your main menu:

| Menu Item | What it's for |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Dashboard** | Process customer exchange transactions |
| **Live Rates** | Monitor market rates and set your store rates/spreads |
| **Transactions** | View and search transaction history |
| **Customers** | Manage your customer database |
| **Level Points** | Set loyalty-tier fees (1–5 stars) |
| **Destinations** | Set up payout locations and their fees |
| **Settings** | Manage currencies and app options |

The **top bar** shows the live clock, your connection status (green = connected),
a **Refresh** button for rates, and a **dark/light theme** toggle.

---

## 1. Process a Transaction (Dashboard)

This is your everyday screen for serving a customer.

1. **Select the customer** from the dropdown (search by name or ID). Their loyalty
   star level is shown next to the name.
2. In **"Customer Pays,"** enter the amount and pick the currency.
3. Pick the currency in **"Customer Receives."** Use the round **swap** button to
   flip the two currencies.
4. The amount the customer receives is calculated automatically, including the
   spread, the customer's loyalty discount, and any destination fee.
5. (Optional) Choose a **Receiving Destination** if the money is being sent out —
   its commission is applied to the total.
6. Review the rate cards (market rate, customer buy rate, customer sell rate).
7. Click **Process Transaction**. You'll get a confirmation with a receipt number.
   Use **Clear** to reset the form.

> Your entries are saved automatically if you navigate away and come back.

---

## 2. Manage Rates (Live Rates)

Keep your store rates accurate and set your profit margin (spread).

- Pick a **Base Currency** and use **Search** to find a pair.
- Compare rates from multiple live sources side by side.
- **Store Rate:** choose **Source** (live feed) or **Custom** (type your own rate).
- **Spread:** choose the type — **% Symmetric**, **% Asymmetric**, **Fixed Symmetric**,
  or **Fixed Asymmetric** — then enter the value(s). Asymmetric lets you set separate
  buy and sell margins.
- **Rounding:** set how many decimals the final rate is rounded to.
- Click **Save** on the row. Turn on **Auto Sync** to refresh rates every 30 seconds,
  or use **Refresh** for a one-time update.

---

## 3. Customers

- **Add one:** click **Add New Customer**, enter name (required), phone, email, and
  star level (1–5), then **Add Customer**.
- **Bulk add:** click **Download Template**, fill in the Excel file, then
  **Upload by Excel**.
- **Search:** filter by name, ID, phone, or email.
- **View / Edit / Delete:** click any customer row to open their detail panel, then
  use **Edit Customer** or **Delete Customer**.

---

## 4. Level Points (Loyalty Fees)

Reward your best customers with lower fees.

1. For each currency pair, choose a fee **Type** — **%** or **Fixed**.
2. Enter a fee for each star level (★ to ★★★★★). **Higher stars should have lower
   fees**; leave blank or 0 for no extra fee.
3. Click **Save** on the row.

These fees apply automatically when you process a transaction on the Dashboard.

---

## 5. Destinations (Payout Locations)

1. Enter a **Name** (e.g., a branch or partner location).
2. Enter the **Commission** and choose **%** or **Fixed**.
3. Click **Add**.
4. Use the **pencil** to edit a row or the **trash** icon to remove it.

Destinations and their fees appear as options on the Dashboard.

---

## 6. Transactions (History)

- See totals at a glance: completed, pending, and volume.
- **Search** by receipt ID, customer, or currency pair.
- Filter by **date range** and **status** (Completed, Pending, Failed, Cancelled).
- Browse pages at the bottom and use **Export CSV** to download a report.

---

## 7. Settings

- **Currencies:** add a currency (code, name, symbol, color), reorder, or remove.
- **Live Rate Fetching:** turn automatic rate updates on/off, or use **Fetch Now**
  for a single update.
- **Optional features:** turn on **Settlement Terms** or **Margins** to add them to
  your sidebar.

---

## Quick Daily Checklist

1. Confirm the top bar shows a **green** connection.
2. Open **Live Rates** → verify rates look right (refresh if needed).
3. Serve customers from the **Dashboard**.
4. Review the day's activity under **Transactions**.
